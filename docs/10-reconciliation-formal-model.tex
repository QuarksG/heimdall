\begin{abstract}
A remittance advice is downstream evidence of upstream business events:
a purchase order was accepted, goods were shipped and counted, an
e-invoice passed validation, quantities and prices were matched, claims
were opened or closed, discounts were applied, and a wire was issued.
This paper defines the inverse function: starting with the remittance
evidence, it reconstructs those events and their accounting or economic
effects, then proves whether they explain the \emph{Giden Havale}
(outgoing wire) to one kuruş. Where one file cannot establish a fact,
a named review state and analyst action---not a guess---is the correct
audit result.
\end{abstract}

\section{Executive propositions and evidentiary status}
The organizing causal chain is:
\[
\text{upstream event}\to\text{remittance evidence}\to
\text{accounting/economic effect}\to\text{reconstructed wire}\to
\text{verdict or analyst action}.
\]
Two propositions govern the paper. First, the \emph{inverse-function
thesis}: every recognized row is the fingerprint of an upstream event
and is mapped back to its cause, lifecycle, and monetary effect. Second,
the \emph{cashier/wire identity}: reconstructed effects must equal the
actual \emph{Giden Havale}. Equality produces GREEN only if independent
controls also pass; otherwise the exact residue or review condition is
named. The model is deterministic: the same admitted workbook evidence
yields the same classification, reconstruction, and verdict.

\section{The inverse-function thesis}
\subsection{Forward process and reverse audit reading}

\textbf{Observed business facts.} Amazon issues a Purchase Order (PO)
identified by an 8-character alphanumeric token, digit-first and
letter-last. The vendor accepts it in Vendor Central. Under Turkish tax
law, the vendor must invoice only goods confirmed as shipped to the
designated Fulfillment Center, no later than eight days after delivery;
cancelled or undelivered items must not be invoiced. In practice,
undelivered goods may nevertheless be invoiced without notice to Amazon.

The FC initially confirms only that goods entered ``Goods Received for
Counting and Scanning.'' Item-level confirmation follows later. This
evidence lag causes the quantity-variance process: Amazon pays against
received goods, not merely invoices, so an unconfirmed portion may be
withheld while evidence remains pending.

The e-invoice travels vendor $\to$ SOVOS $\to$ GIS $\to$ VPS. SOVOS
performs GİB signature and regulatory checks; GIS routes to the legal
entity and business line, here Retail; VPS/FinOps performs commercial
validation and tax or legislative classification. Failed validation
receives a defect category and is parked for analyst action.

\textbf{Audit consequence.} A remittance row has passed upstream
authenticity, regulatory, commercial, and tax controls. This model does
not re-adjudicate authenticity; it tests arithmetic, references,
lifecycle, and settlement effect.

Retail payment validation is a three-way match among PO-confirmed goods,
goods received after counting and scanning, and invoiced quantities and
prices. A full match schedules payment on the due date (invoice date plus
payment term). Contractual deductions, principally CCOGS, are taken at
execution. A partial quantity match creates a Shortage Claim
(\texttt{SC}) debit and an \texttt{SCR} credit process seeking receipt
evidence through due date $+60$ days. Matched amounts accumulate for the
vendor payment cycle, typically seven days. Price mismatches create the
parallel \texttt{PC}/\texttt{PCR} process for PPV.

\textbf{Observed timing fact.} An SC posts in OFA on the parent invoice's
due date, not on an FC event date. Every remittance row is a transaction
that occurred. Therefore row age and claim window are reconstructible
from the file. The AP side records PQV (SC/SCR) and PPV (PC/PCR); the AR
side records CCOGS, vendor returns (VRET), advertising deductions,
non-invoiced provisions, and QPD.

\section{Remittance evidence and admission controls}
\subsection{Workbook as stable evidence}
A worksheet is a finite matrix
\[
M\in(V\cup\{\varnothing\})^{R\times C},
\]
with each cell at a unique coordinate $(r,c)$. Let $D$ be the issuer's
invariant disclaimer and let normalization be fixed uppercasing with
Turkish-aware folding. The documentary anchor is
\[
a=(r_0,c_0)=\min_{\text{row-major}}
\{(r,c):\operatorname{normalize}(M[r,c])\supseteq D\}.
\]
Header and invoice evidence are read relative to the anchor. The first
worksheet is the stated evidence scope; additional sheets require a
warning and analyst instruction.

\subsection{Admission and degradation rules}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
\item If $D$ is absent from the first $K$ rows, no amount is read; the
file is rejected with a bilingual, actionable reason.
\item A section lacking a payment marker is excluded with its row named;
other sections remain reviewable.
\item Header evidence comprises \emph{Ödeme yapılacak taraf, Tedarikçi
Numaranız, Tedarikçi site adı, Ödeme numarası, Ödeme tarihi, Ödeme para
birimi, Ödeme tutarı}.
\item Invoice evidence comprises \emph{Fatura Numarası, Fatura Tarihi,
Fatura Açıklaması, Uygulanan indirim, Ödenen Tutar, Kalan Tutar}.
\item Every positional fallback discloses its coordinate and assumed
meaning; no heuristic or exclusion is silent.
\item Parenthesized paid amounts are debits; bare amounts are credits.
Source punctuation uses comma thousands and dot decimals. An unreadable
amount becomes zero and is named by the completeness control.
\item Dates admit \texttt{D-MMM-YY(YY)}, a spreadsheet serial in
$[20000,80000]$, or a last-resort generic date. Failure is null, never
day zero, and gives a claim an anomaly state.
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Invariant---loud degradation.} Every exclusion, heuristic,
worksheet limitation, unreadable field, or fallback names its coordinate,
group, or reason.
\section{Monetary axioms and structural controls}
\subsection{Money model}
For each row $x$:
\[
\roundtwo(x)=\frac{\lfloor100x\rceil}{100},\qquad
\cash(x)=\operatorname{credit}(x)-\operatorname{debit}(x),\qquad
\gross(x)=\cash(x)+\ind(x),
\]
\[
a\approx b\iff |a-b|\le\eps,\qquad \eps=0.01.
\]
Here cash is \emph{Alacak $-$ Borç} and $\ind$ is \emph{Uygulanan
indirim}.

\begin{axiom}[Net-of-discount withholding]
Cash columns are net of QPD. A discount clawback appears in $\ind(x)$
with negative sign. Therefore $\gross(x)$ is the vendor-facing economic
value. The tolerance is one kuruş, a numerical guard rather than a
business threshold.
\end{axiom}

\subsection{Age and structural validation}
\[
\operatorname{age}(x)=\operatorname{days}
(\operatorname{invoiceDate}(x),\operatorname{paymentDate}(x)).
\]
Sales-row age reveals the payment term. An SC payment date starts its
claim window. Large age outliers identify delayed settlement; the full
column is the aged report, and a bimodal profile flags mixed populations.
The vendor site has eight characters in \texttt{ORG\_CC} form; for
example \texttt{ZL7LD\_TR} identifies organization ZL7LD in Türkiye.
The payment number has exactly nine digits. Reversal numbers must extend
an existing document number. Notification/reversal families must show
the discount claw/give-back pattern. Rows without amounts are named and
linked to any simultaneous payment-total mismatch.

\subsection{Independent payment-total control}
For $g=(\text{payment number},\text{payment date})$, declared total
$P(g)$, and row-derived net $N(g)$:
\[
N(g)=\sum_{x\in g}\cash(x),\qquad
\bigl|P(g)-|N(g)|\bigr|\le\eps.
\]
The header and table are independent evidence. Failure names the group
and delta. An audit presentation row \emph{Giden Havale} is placed on
the side opposite $N(g)$ with magnitude $|N(g)|$, so
\[
\sum\operatorname{credit}-\sum\operatorname{debit}
-\operatorname{transfer}(g)=0.
\]
It is excluded from the independent control because it is derived from
$N(g)$.

\subsection{Verified sales and series inference}
A sale is verified iff (i) $\operatorname{len}(n)\le16$; (ii) its
description contains \texttt{\{PO\}/\{warehouse\}/}, with PO exactly
eight alphanumerics, digit-first and letter-last, and warehouse in the
closed code set; and (iii) the path tail is empty or begins with an exact
echo of $n$.

\begin{ruling}[A1]
This predicate has no known false positives in the source population and
is the only evidence strong enough to support file-specific inference.
\end{ruling}

Let $V$ be verified sales and $V_c$ those beginning with $c$:
\[
\operatorname{own}(c)\iff |V|\ge m\ \wedge\ \frac{|V_c|}{|V|}\ge\theta,
\qquad m=5,\quad\theta=0.33.
\]
When owned, bare-prefix cooperation or return rules move from precedence
13/14 to 25/26 immediately before fallback; evidence branches never
move. The status is disclosed with counts or the exact reason it is
inactive. A cited token equal to the row's number is a self-echo, not
evidence. If a description contains, in order, \texttt{FOR TRANSACTION},
a V-token consisting of V, one digit, and 14 alphanumerics, then
\texttt{DSPT}, it is an \emph{İade Faturası} regardless of own prefix,
at precedence 12.5 above cooperation.

\section{Claim lifecycle: rounds, windows, and states}
\subsection{Grammar, root, and round}
Quantity and price claims share one lifecycle:
\[
\operatorname{number}=B\cdot\operatorname{tail},\qquad
\operatorname{tail}=((\mathtt{SC}\mid\mathtt{PC})
(\mathtt{R}\mid\mathtt{RI})?)^+.
\]
$B$ is the wholesale root. The root rule removes the longest parseable
mixed tail because families may interleave, as in \texttt{...SCRPCRI}.
The terminal token assigns family: \texttt{SC*} to PQV and \texttt{PC*}
to PPV.

One root may contain independent rounds. For family token $\tau$, a
deduction $d$ ends in $\tau$; only $d\mathtt{R}$ (RELEASED) or
$d\mathtt{RI}$ (VALIDATED) closes it, with validation prevailing if both
exist. Finals are IQV/IPV documents paired by reference. Rounds order by
deduction-number length. A round is referential, never amount-matched,
because the discount axiom makes valid cash amounts unequal.

A final carries \texttt{RI|family|seg1|seg2}. Pairing uses \texttt{seg1}
only; in round one it is the root. \texttt{seg2} is always the root and
must never pair, or later and sibling finals would attach to round one.
An unresolved final may attach to the sole validated round still awaiting
a final; otherwise it is UNATTACHED---Review Final Invoice.

\subsection{Round arithmetic and window}
For round $r$ and claim rows $C(r)$:
\begin{align*}
\operatorname{net}(r)&=\sum_{x\in C(r)}\cash(x),&
\operatorname{disc}(r)&=\sum_{x\in C(r)}\ind(x),\\
\operatorname{res}(r)&=\roundtwo(\operatorname{net}(r)+
\operatorname{disc}(r)),&
K_1(r)&:\ |\operatorname{res}(r)|\le\eps,\\
G(r)&=-\sum_{x\in\operatorname{ded}(r)}\gross(x),&
\operatorname{ipv}(r)&=\sum_{\text{per document}}
(\operatorname{debit}-\operatorname{credit}),\\
K_2(r)&:\ |\operatorname{ipv}(r)-G(r)|\le\eps.&&
\end{align*}
Installments under one final-document number are allowed. Window terms are
\[
\operatorname{due}(r)=\text{own deduction payment date},\quad
H=\max\text{ parseable payment date},\quad
 e(r)=\operatorname{days}(\operatorname{due}(r),H),\quad L=63.
\]
Thus $L$ is due date $+60$-day policy $+72$-hour creation lag; the
historical predecessor was invoice date $+33$. Every round uses its own
deduction date.

\subsection{Business scenarios and analyst actions}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.5em,style=nextline]
\item[S1---Open inside window.] Money is in suspense, not established
loss, though overdue in the vendor ledger. Inform the vendor that
matching continues.
\item[S2---Open beyond window.] A dispute condition. Causes include a
balance below the remittance trigger while the claim aged, one-sided
cancellation in SC state contrary to policy, or another unprocessed
condition. Analyst action is mandatory.
\item[S3---Converted.] Final IQV/IPV is present and $K_1\wedge K_2$
holds. This is a lawful confirmed variance, including invoiced goods
that never arrived.
\item[S4---Closure without in-file deduction.] A root knock-out needs no
attention. A positive payout requires a prior-period search. If no prior
deduction exists, track the excess and expected future clawback.
\item[S5---QPD-active.] Deduct-net/release-gross inequality is intended;
apply the QPD algebra rather than cash comparison.
\end{description}

\section{QPD discount algebra}
\subsection{Learned-rate overlay}
QPD economics occur in affected rows' $\ind(x)$, not only QPD-labelled
rows. Deductions withhold net; reversals return gross. For wholesale root
$B$, face value $F=\gross(B)$, and first-claim magnitude $G_1$, the rate
is learned per file:
\[
r=\frac{\ind(B)}{F},\qquad
q_1=rG_1=-\ind(\mathrm{SC}_1),\qquad
\operatorname{reversal}_1=G_1\ (\text{gross},\ind=0),
\]
\[
\text{rounds }k\ge2:\quad \ind=0,\quad\cash=\gross.
\]

\subsection{Worked example: AND2026000010288 at 5.00\%}
\begin{align*}
\text{root: }&\cash=+168{,}771.11,\quad\ind=+8{,}882.69
&&\Rightarrow F=177{,}653.80,\\
\text{SC: }&\cash=-129{,}261.69,\quad\ind=-6{,}803.25
&&\Rightarrow -G_1=-136{,}064.94,\\
r&=8{,}882.69/177{,}653.80=5.00\%,\\
q_1&=5.00\%\times136{,}064.94=6{,}803.25=-\ind(\mathrm{SC}),\\
\operatorname{reversal}_1&=136{,}064.94\quad\text{gross},\\
K_1&:\ +6{,}803.25-6{,}803.25=0,\\
K_2&:\ \operatorname{iqv}(B)=15{,}100.34=G(d_{\mathrm{last}}).
\end{align*}
Settlement decomposition:
\begin{align*}
F-G_1&=177{,}653.80-136{,}064.94=41{,}588.86,\\
\operatorname{QPDdoc}(B)&=\sum\ind\text{ over }\{B\}\cup C(B)
=8{,}882.69-6{,}803.25=2{,}079.44\\
&=5.00\%\times41{,}588.86,\\
\operatorname{CASH}(B)&=F-\operatorname{iqv}(B)-
\operatorname{QPDdoc}(B)\\
&=177{,}653.80-15{,}100.34-2{,}079.44=160{,}474.02.
\end{align*}
Cash equals face value less confirmed shortage and discount retained on
the paid portion. Every term, including the rate, comes from the file.

\begin{align*}
\operatorname{legit}(B)&=\sum\ind\text{ over root and claim rows;
reversals carry zero},\\
\operatorname{qpdNet}(B)&=\sum(\operatorname{debit}-
\operatorname{credit})\text{ over debit QPD documents, grouped by root}.
\end{align*}

\section{The cashier/wire identity}
The cashier identity translates reconstructed effects into the amount
that should have been wired and compares it with \emph{Giden Havale}.

\subsection{Layer 1: type aggregation and conservation}
Per currency and type $t$:
\[
\operatorname{Fark}(t)=\sum\operatorname{credit}-
\sum\operatorname{debit}.
\]
\begin{theorem}[Fark conservation]
\[
\sum_t\operatorname{Fark}(t)=\sum_x\cash(x).
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Both sides sum every row exactly once under a total, disjoint
classification.
\end{proof}
Failure invalidates reconstruction. The aggregation reports the minimum
and maximum parseable sales-invoice date. Types show how much effect
exists; rounds identify which invoices carry it.

\subsection{Layer 2: reconstructed wire}
\begin{longtable}{@{}p{0.28\textwidth}p{0.64\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Component} & \textbf{Formula and basis}\\
\midrule
\endhead

DEDUCTION$_{IPV}$ & Exception: $\sum\gross$ over IPV---gross\\

\end{longtable}
\[
\operatorname{Computed}=\sum\text{components},\quad
\operatorname{Actual}=\sum(\operatorname{debit}-\operatorname{credit})
\text{ over Giden Havale},
\]
\[
\operatorname{Diff}=\operatorname{Computed}-\operatorname{Actual}.
\]
Define
\[
\text{QPD-mismatch}\iff
\left|\sum\operatorname{debit}_{QPD}-\sum\ind(\text{all})\right|>\eps,
\]
\[
\operatorname{GATE}=\mathrm{GREEN}\iff
|\operatorname{Diff}|\le\eps\ \wedge\ \neg\text{QPD-mismatch}.
\]
A QPD mismatch forces RED even at zero difference. Only Pending Matching
and Stuck chains contribute as open chains. Anomaly, Slips, Excess
Credit, and Review Final Invoice remain unresolved rather than being
absorbed.
\subsection{Open-claw lemma}
\begin{lemma}
If open claims enter at cash instead of gross,
\[
\operatorname{Diff}_{cash}-\operatorname{Diff}_{gross}
=-\sum\ind(x)\quad\text{over open claim rows}.
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
An open deduction withholds cash net of the QPD claw, while
$\mathrm{KEEP\_QPD}=-\sum\ind(\text{all})$ already includes that claw.
Cash basis understates the withholding by the same amount. Gross basis
restores it, so the effects cancel.
\end{proof}
Two observed open rounds carried claws of 549.01 and 617.63. Cash basis
produced a permanent RED difference of 1,166.64; gross basis closed to
zero. Gross also equals the vendor-facing \emph{Kalıntı (Brüt)} total.

\subsection{Layer 3: ledger closure}
The ledger admits all target, included, kept, and unresolved rows, plus
only rows owned by open chains for open-items-only types. Every source
record appears at most once and remains in source order. Because ledger
rows carry no discount, GREEN closes to an offset rather than raw zero:
\begin{align*}
\operatorname{offset}={}&\operatorname{salesDiscount}
+\operatorname{ipvDiscount}+\operatorname{openScScrDiscount}\\
&+\operatorname{openPcPcrDiscount}
-\sum\ind(\text{all})-\cash(\text{QPD rows}).
\end{align*}
\begin{theorem}[Ledger closure]
\[
\mathrm{GREEN}\Rightarrow
\sum(\mathrm{Borç}-\mathrm{Alacak})\text{ over ledger}
\approx\operatorname{offset}.
\]
\end{theorem}
Failure invalidates the claimed GREEN result.

\section{Audit invariants}
\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.5em,style=nextline]
\item[I1---Determinism.] Every classification, derivation, and verdict is
a pure function of admitted workbook evidence.
\item[I2---Conservation.] No owned row vanishes; every row belongs to one
chain; chain nets reproduce type totals; unpairable documents are flagged.
\item[I3---Row uniqueness.] No record is counted twice across identity,
open items, or ledger.
\item[I4---Explained or flagged.] Every row is closed, open inside its
window, or in a named review/anomaly state. Sınıflandırılmamış is a flag,
not an absorber.
\item[I5---Loud degradation.] Every lossy or heuristic evidence step
names its coordinate, group, or reason.
\end{description}
A numerically balancing file is not auditable if conservation,
uniqueness, classification visibility, or QPD consistency fails.
